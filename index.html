Event Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential answers regarding team eligibility, registration schedule, and challenge architecture.

What is CHAKRAVYUH?
CHAKRAVYUH: CYBERSHIELD HACKATHON is the largest offline hackathon at VIT-AP University, bringing together student developers to design cyber-resilient solutions across five technology frontiers.
When and where is the event conducted?
The hackathon is conducted offline on 27–28 October 2026 at the VIT-AP University campus near Amaravati / Vijayawada.
What are the three challenge tiers (Flagship, Medium, Easy)?
Each of the five frontiers offers three distinct difficulty tiers: Flagship (advanced, high-impact architecture), Medium (applied problem-solving), and Easy (foundational challenges).
Is On-Duty (OD) leave provided for participants?
Yes, official On-Duty (OD) attendance approval is provided for all confirmed participating students.
